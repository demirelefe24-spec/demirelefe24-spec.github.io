  3. Event30–31 October 2025

    Ordu Apiculture Research Institute: AI in Beekeeping Workshop

    We attended as invited guests and presented our work on automating larva transfer. The workshop report includes BeeBal's work.

  4. IP2025

    34th in Türkiye among funded startups with patent or IP protection

    In 2025 we ranked 34th among Turkish technology startups that had raised investment and held patent or IP protection.

  5. IP2025

    Utility model: Autonomous Beekeeping Container

    Our utility model application for an “Autonomous Beekeeping Container Enabling Fully Automatic Production of Bee Products” was approved. Application no. 2025/004528.

  6. Award2025

    AAKRUTI Global 2025

    In the global engineering and innovation competition run by Dassault Systèmes and SOLIDWORKS we were selected among the Türkiye finalists.

  7. Award2025

    InovaTIM 2025: first place, Sustainability and Green Technologies

    Out of more than 2,000 applications, with our Autonomous Royal Jelly Machine, which aims to automate larva transfer and royal jelly harvesting.

  8. Event2024 and 2025

    Take Off Istanbul

    Two years in a row, under Yildiz Technopark, we presented our autonomous beekeeping technology to investors and technology leaders.

  9. Programme2024–2025

    Yildiz Kasifleri, first cohort

    We were selected for YTU Yildiz Technopark's early-stage programme and received equipment and mentoring support. Our first prototypes were built during it.

  10. Award2024

    14th International Beekeeping Fair: first place for innovation

    The first time we showed the prototype directly to beekeepers. One of them said: “If it goes on sale, we want to be among your first users.”

  11. Event20 May 2023

    World Bee Day, Balıkesir

    At the Beekeepers' Association event we talked one to one with beekeepers from across Türkiye. Most of the ideas we work on today came out of those conversations.

  12. Company

    TÜBİTAK BIGG investment and incorporation

    We earned the Seal of Excellence in the TÜBİTAK BIGG programme, raised TRY 900,000 for a 3% stake and incorporated as BEEBAL TEKNOLOJİ A.Ş.

  13. Company

    Trademark application

    We filed an official trademark application for “BeeBal Otonom Arıcılık Teknolojileri” and its logo.

  14. Company

    Tech Startup Badge

    We received the Ministry of Industry and Technology's badge for technology-driven, scalable startups.

  15. Programme

    TEKYAZ: SOLIDWORKS licence support

    With a SOLIDWORKS licence from TEKYAZ Eğitim we run our mechanical design and prototyping on professional tools. Our story was also published on the TEKYAZ Blog.
