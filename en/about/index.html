Bringing the work of bees and technology together

BeeBal is a technology startup that combines traditional beekeeping with advanced engineering to build autonomous machines.

Our story

BeeBal's five co-founders are graduates of ARGEM, Türkiye's first high school for gifted students. During their boarding-school years they built projects in different fields, took part in science olympiads and ran peer mentoring. That range of backgrounds underpins the team's work today.

BeeBal's story begins with the family of co-founder Furkan Keskin. His father, Mümin Keskin, and the family have kept bees on Madra Mountain in Balıkesir for many years. Growing up, Furkan Keskin saw the problems beekeepers face at first hand: colony management, heavy labour, lost yield and the margin for error in delicate work. At university he realised technology could address them and shared the idea with Ayşenur Açıkgöz, Efe Şam, Hasan Yahya Kuyumcu and Muhammed Ali Karslı.

From idea to machine

  1. 1

    First: mobile app ideas

    We started with digital tools for beekeepers. Talking to them, we saw these had no real place in the field.

  2. 2

    Then: autonomous beekeeping on a trailer

    We designed a system to ease hive moving for migratory beekeepers; it proved hard to make practical.

  3. 3

    Today: OTAK

    The Autonomous Beekeeping Container, bringing different beekeeping needs under one roof. Its utility model was approved.

  4. 4

    First product: Larva Transfer Machine

    A module of OTAK designed to be sold on its own too, which makes it our market-entry product.

Our long-term goal: technology that makes beekeeping more accessible and productive.

In the long run we want to build technology that makes beekeeping more accessible and productive, for local producers and for the global beekeeping community.
